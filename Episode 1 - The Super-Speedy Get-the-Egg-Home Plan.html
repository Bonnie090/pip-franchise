Pip Has a Plan!

Episode 1: "The Super-Speedy Get-the-Egg-Home Plan" · Running time 7:00 · Ages 4–8 · Draft 1

At a glance

LoglineAt Puddle Park, Pip and his friends find a strange, giant, speckled thing that wobbles and goes tap-tap-tap. It's an egg! Pip makes a super-speedy plan to get it home, but he skips step four: finding out whose egg it is.
LessonSlow down and listen. Start at the beginning (the green dot).
CastPip, Tuck, Poppy, Skye. Cameos: Mama Fern, Dot, Papa Rowan. New: Mrs. Honkaby (a goose), her gosling, two ducks. Silent: Sunny the Sun, Puff and Fluff, the daisies.
LocationsPip's Den (front door), Puddle Park (new, beside Circle Pond), Circle Pond, the hill above the park, Plan HQ (tag).
Format noteThe bible's standard story is 11 minutes. This is a 7-minute version of the same 10-beat structure.

Script

1. Exterior. Pip's Den, morning 0:00–0:30

Bright morning in Daisy Dell. SUNNY THE SUN yawns and stretches over the hills. The round teal front door bursts open. PIP zooms out, bouncing on his toes, his tan satchel bouncing on his hip, pencil peeking out of the top. He pats the satchel: pencil? Check.

PipPark day! Park day! PARK DAAAY!

MAMA FERN leans out of the door, wiping flour off her paws. Baby DOT peeks out behind her, holding her stubby crayon like Pip's pencil.

Mama FernStay with your friends, and stay where Papa can see you. He's delivering mail right by the park gate.

Pip(already running)I will! I'm five-and-a-little-bit! I'm basically a big kid!

Dot(waving her crayon)Pip! Plan!

Pip skids, runs back, gives Dot a quick hug, and zooms off again, leaving a little dust puff. Mama smiles and shakes her head.

MAIN TITLE: the Pip Has a Plan! logo, drawn by Pip's pencil along a dotted line from a green dot. Short "Pip-Pip-Hooray!" theme sting.

2. Exterior. Puddle Park gate 0:30–1:00

A cheerful little park beside Circle Pond: a big old willow tree, a slide made from a hollow log, vine swings, a sandbox, and lots of shiny puddles. PAPA ROWAN rides up on his red bicycle and parks by the mailbox at the gate. He tips his teal cap.

Papa RowanMorning, park patrol! I'll be right here at the mailbox.

TUCK walks up slowly, reading a little booklet called PARK RULES, his red glasses sliding down his nose. POPPY jogs in place, purple sweatband on. SKYE flutters down to a low branch of the willow.

PoppyRace you to the willow tree!

PipYou're ON!

Pip and Poppy blast off. Tuck keeps walking at exactly the same speed, still reading.

TuckPark rule number one: no running near the... (they're gone) ...puddles.

SPLASH. Pip and Poppy both skid through a puddle. Poppy wins by a nose. Her spines puff up proudly.

PoppyHa! Winner!

Pip(dripping, cheerful)I was going for "wettest." I won that one!

3. Exterior. Puddle Park, under the willow 1:00–1:45

Skye hops along her low branch, peering at the bushes below.

Skye(softly)Um... everyone? There's something under the bush.

The gang creeps over and pushes the leaves aside. Half-hidden in the grass is something BIG: round, smooth, pale cream with soft brown speckles, almost as tall as Tuck. The daisies around it lean away and peek through their petals.

Pip(whispering, wide-eyed)Whoa. It's... it's... what IS it?

PoppyIt's a ball! A giant spotty ball! I'm gonna kick it!

Tuck(alarmed)Don't kick it! We don't know what it is!

PipMaybe it's a moon that fell down! Or a dinosaur's marble! Or a... a giant potato!

The thing WOBBLES. Everyone freezes. Tap. Tap-tap. Tap-tap-tap. Pip's tail does the PUFF-UP: it swells to three times its size, every hair sticking straight out. He hides behind it.

Pip(from behind his tail, voice cracking high)I'm not scared!

His enormous puffed tail slowly turns around to look at him. Pip pats it down, embarrassed. Tuck leans in close, pushing up his glasses.

TuckDid you know... that it's not a ball, or a moon, or a potato? It's an EGG. A great big egg. And did you know eggs need to stay nice and warm, with their mama?

Skye(worried)But where's its mama?

Everyone looks around. No mama anywhere. PUFF and FLUFF, the twin clouds, look at each other with worried faces. Pip's ears droop for a second, then shoot straight up.

4. Exterior. Puddle Park, continuous 1:45–2:15

Pip flips open his satchel and whips out his pencil and a pad of sticky notes. He sticks out his tongue (plan face) and scribbles furiously.

PipOoh! I have a PLAN! The Super-Speedy Get-the-Egg-Home Plan!

PoppyOoh, super-speedy. I like it already.

PipEggs live in nests. Nests are near water. Water is in Circle Pond. So this egg lives at Circle Pond! It's only, like, a hundred tail-lengths away!

Plan Vision

5. Plan Vision: workbook page 2:15–2:50

The screen becomes a cream workbook page. Everything is a gray pencil sketch, with only Pip's orange and teal colored in like crayon. A green start dot. Dotted arrows. Pip narrates like a sports announcer.

Pip (V.O.)Step ONE: Pip finds a wagon! (a sketch-Pip pulls a wagon from behind the slide)

Pip (V.O.)Step TWO: Pip lifts the egg, super gently, onto his cozy scarf! (sketch-Pip tucks his scarf around the egg)

Pip (V.O.)Step THREE: Pip rolls it to Circle Pond, ZOOM! (the wagon zooms along the dotted line)

Pip (V.O.)Step FIVE: the egg is HOME! And the crowd goes WILD! (sketch-ducks cheer, sketch-Pip does the arms-up victory pose)

Big circled numbers on the page: 1, 2, 3, 5. There is clearly no 4. An eraser wipes the sketch away, and we're back in full color.

6. Exterior. Puddle Park 2:50–3:10

Tuck(raising one finger)Um, Pip? What about step four?

PipThere IS no step four!

Tuck...That's what I mean.

But Pip is already gone, bouncing toward the hollow-log slide. Skye flutters after him.

Skye(quietly)Pip, I saw something next to the egg, it was a—

Pip(not hearing her, pulling out an old wagon)STEP ONE! Found a wagon! Pip is on FIRE today, folks!

Skye's shoulders droop. She sits back down on a low branch, holding something small and white behind her back: a big white feather. (Hold on it for a moment, so kids at home notice it.)

7. Exterior. Puddle Park, little hill 3:10–4:15

STEP TWO: Pip, Tuck and Poppy carefully lift the egg onto the wagon. Pip takes off his teal scarf and tucks it around the egg like a blanket. The scarf star sits right on top.

PipThere. Nice and cozy.

PipSTEP THREE! Roll it to Circle Pond! Super-speedy!

Pip pulls the wagon fast, faster, over the top of a little grassy hill. The wagon tips over the top and starts to roll down... by itself. The handle slips out of Pip's paws.

PipUh-oh.

EveryoneUH-OHHH!

CHASE! The wagon bumps and rattles down the gentle hill toward the sandbox. Pip runs after it, ears flat, arms pumping, tail streaming behind. The daisies cover their eyes. Sunny peeks through his fingers.

Pip(running, sports announcer)And the wagon is getting away, folks! This is NOT part of the plan!

PoppyI got this! Race you, wagon!

Poppy zooms past everyone, the fastest in the park, and gets ahead of the wagon. She skids to a stop in front of it and... curls into a spiky ball out of habit.

Tuck(horrified)Poppy, NO! Spiky plus egg equals BAD!

Poppy(uncurling fast)Oops! Right! Not spiky! Not spiky!

She dives sideways and kicks a big pile of soft raked leaves in front of the wagon. FWUMP! The wagon plows into the leaves and stops. The egg bounces up... and lands safely back on Pip's scarf. Leaves flutter down everywhere. Everyone breathes out.

Pip(panting, one leaf on his head)Okay... Plan B.

TuckPlan B is: we walk. Slowly.

Pip...Plan B is: we walk slowly.

Quick comic montage: the gang tiptoes the wagon toward the pond in super slow motion. Pip bounces on his toes the whole way, trying so hard to be slow. A snail passes them. Puff and Fluff exchange a look.

8. Exterior. Circle Pond, the grassy edge 4:15–4:45

They arrive at the round pond, staying on the grass. Two DUCKS paddle by, close to the edge.

Pip(proudly)Step FIVE! Ta-da! We found your egg!

The ducks look at the giant egg. They look at each other. They look at the egg again.

Duck 1Oh, sweetie, that's not ours.

Duck 2(holding up a wing to measure)Our eggs are THIS big. That egg is... THAT big.

The ducks paddle away. Silence. The egg goes tap... tap..., weaker this time.

9. Exterior. Circle Pond, continuous 4:45–5:10

Everything slows down. Pip's ears fold back. His tail droops all the way to the ground. He sits down next to the wagon. The music goes quiet.

Pip(small voice)I just wanted to help it get home. And now it's even farther away.

Tuck sits down beside him. Poppy, too. Nobody says "I told you so."

10. Exterior. Circle Pond, continuous 5:10–5:35

Skye flutters down and lands right in front of Pip. She takes a big brave breath.

SkyePip? I tried to tell you before. When we found the egg... I found THIS.

She holds out the big white feather.

SkyeIt's a feather. A really big one. Bigger than a duck feather.

Pip looks at the feather. Then at Skye. He really LISTENS this time. His left ear lifts. Then his right ear lifts. Then his tail does one... slow... wag.

PipSkye. You were trying to tell me the most important step. And I didn't listen. I'm sorry.

Skye(shy, happy)That's okay.

Pip(standing, ears up)Step four: find out WHOSE egg it is! And to do that... we have to go back to where we started.

Tuck(smiling)Back to the green dot.

11. Exterior. Puddle Park, under the willow 5:35–6:15

Back at the bush where they found the egg. This time Pip goes slowly. He kneels down and looks closely, and there it is: a little trail of big white feathers, one... two... three... leading away up the hill, like a dotted line.

Pip(whispering, delighted)It's a dotted path! A feather path!

They follow it together, slowly and carefully, pulling the wagon. The feather trail ends at the bottom of a grassy rise covered in tall reeds. Too tall to see over.

PoppyI can't see anything!

TuckMe neither. Turtles are very good at many things. Seeing over reeds is not one of them.

Everyone looks at Skye. Skye looks up at a branch, a little higher than she usually goes. She gulps. Pip steps beside her.

Pip(gently)You don't have to. But we'll be right here.

Skye flaps her too-big wings, wobbles... and flutters up, up, onto the higher branch. She looks out. Her eyes go wide.

SkyeI can see it from up here! There's a big nest, and a big white goose, and she looks SO worried!

They hear a frantic HONK! HONK!

12. Exterior. The goose nest in the reeds 6:15–6:45

The gang pushes the wagon through the reeds into a clearing. MRS. HONKABY, a big, soft, white goose with a flowered hat, is searching under every leaf. She sees the egg, wrapped in Pip's scarf, and gasps.

Mrs. HonkabyMy egg! Oh, my baby egg! It rolled away when the wind blew, and I've been looking EVERYWHERE!

She gently tucks the egg back into her nest and settles over it. She hands Pip back his scarf. The egg goes TAP-TAP-TAP! A crack appears. Everyone leans in. Pip starts to bounce on his toes.

PipWait for it... wait for it...

Beat. Everyone looks at Pip, expecting him to rush. But this time... he waits. He actually WAITS. He holds Skye's wing and Tuck's hand, and stays perfectly still. (Big moment of growth, played small and sweet.)

CRACK! A fluffy yellow GOSLING pops out, with a bit of shell on its head like a hat. It blinks at Pip.

GoslingPeep!

Pip(gasping)He said my NAME!

TuckDid you know baby geese say "peep"?

Pip(certain)He said PIP.

The gosling throws both wings up in the air, copying Pip's victory pose. Pip throws his arms up too. Everyone does.

Pip, Tuck, Poppy and SkyePip-pip-HOORAY!

Sunny beams. Puff and Fluff high-five. The daisies cheer silently.

13. Interior. Plan HQ, late afternoon 6:45–7:00

Golden light. Pip sticks a new sticky note on the Plan Wall: THE SUPER-SPEEDY GET-THE-EGG-HOME PLAN. He pulls his pencil out of his satchel and carefully adds a new line between 3 and 5.

Pip(writing, tongue out)Step four... LISTEN to Skye.

Skye beams and her daisy bounces. Tuck pushes up his glasses, proud. Poppy gives Pip a high-five. Then a tiny "Peep!" at the window: the gosling waddles past outside with Mrs. Honkaby, wings up in the victory pose. Pip waves.

PipSee you tomorrow, little buddy!

Pip's tail swishes happily. Iris out on the yellow star on his scarf.

END OF EPISODE.

Safety check
  • A grown-up (Papa) is nearby the whole time, and Mama sets the rule to stay together.
  • The kids stay on the grass at the pond's edge and never go in the water.
  • Nobody rides the runaway wagon. It rolls down a gentle hill into soft leaves.
  • The egg is returned to its mama. Nobody keeps a wild animal.
New for the bible
  • Puddle Park: a little park beside Circle Pond with a big willow, a hollow-log slide, vine swings, a sandbox and lots of puddles.
  • Mrs. Honkaby: a big, soft, white goose with a flowered hat who nests in the reeds near the park.
  • The gosling: says "Peep!" (which Pip hears as "Pip!") and copies Pip's victory pose. A possible recurring little friend.
  • Two ducks at Circle Pond (background characters).
